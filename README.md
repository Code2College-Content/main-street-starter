# Main Street Hardware & Feed: sales data (pair repo)

Fictional business, made for practice in the Code2College apprenticeship.
This repo is public. Never put personal info here: no real names, no phone
numbers, no passwords, no real client data.

## Dale's ask

"Every spring I over-order fencing and run out of seed. I know the answer is
in the sales history, I just can't see it. Show me what sells when, and tell
me what to stock next season."

## What is here

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `data/raw/` | Dale's monthly sales exports from the register. Do not change these. |
| `data/register_totals.csv` | The register's own monthly report. Our independent check. Do not change it. |
| `count_check.md` | Your Count check card. Fill it in. |
| `report.html` | The report page you finish in Session 8. |
| `.github/copilot-instructions.md` | Rules Copilot follows in this repo. |

## Today's deliverable (Session 7)

* `cleaned_sales.csv` and `monthly_totals.csv` at the top of the repo
* `count_check.md` filled in
* One sentence for Dale about what you changed, in his words

## Pair rules

1. One Codespace drives at a time. The driver types, the navigator reads every
   plan and every command out loud before anyone clicks Allow.
2. Swap halfway: the driver commits and syncs, then the partner opens this
   repo in their own Codespace, syncs, and drives.
3. Sync before you start. Commit and sync when you stop.

## Stop your Codespace when you leave

Codespaces menu (bottom left), then Stop Current Codespace. It saves your free hours.
